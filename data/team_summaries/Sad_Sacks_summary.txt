## Sad Sacks Strengths
- **Elite starting pitching**: Chris Sale ($26.78 netValue), George Kirby ($17.52), Joey Cantillo ($2.34) provide strong W/K foundation
- **Closers**: Jhoan Duran ($19.07) elite in both HLD and SV categories
- **Five-category SS**: Bobby Witt Jr. ($18.29) - elite in all hitting categories including 34.5 SB
- **OF depth**: Ward ($16.18), Marsee ($9.44), Kwan ($5.51) - three protectable OFs
- **Steals production**: Witt (34.5), Marsee (33.1), Kwan (17.7) give strong SB foundation
- **Catcher**: Shea Langeliers ($12.12) - strong power numbers, scarce position

## Sad Sacks Weaknesses
- **1B position**: Isaac Paredes ($1.51) barely protectable, weak production
- **No 3B**: Complete positional gap
- **Relief depth**: Only Brad Keller ($3.15) beyond Duran for holds/saves
- **2B depth**: Only Gleyber Torres, no backup
- **Age concerns**: Sale (37), Ward (33), Correa (32), Keller (31) all on older side
- **SS redundancy**: Both Witt and Correa - one is expendable

## Their Trade Chips
- **Carlos Correa** (SS, $6.01) - blocked by Witt, solid production but redundant
- **Steven Kwan** (OF, $5.51) - third OF, could be moved if consolidating
- **Brad Keller** (MR, $3.15) - marginal value, expendable
- **Isaac Paredes** (1B, $1.51) - weakest protectable position player
- **Gleyber Torres** (2B, $11.76) - if they get a better 2B back

## What They're Looking For
- **3B upgrade**: Glaring positional need with no one rostered
- **1B upgrade**: Paredes is their weakest protectable hitter
- **High-value consolidation**: Need to turn 13 protectables into 12 through 2-for-1 or 3-for-2 deals
- **Relief help**: Additional HLD/SV depth beyond Duran/Keller
- **Youth/upside**: Given age concerns with Sale/Ward/Correa
