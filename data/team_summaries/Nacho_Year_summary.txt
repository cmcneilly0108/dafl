# TRADE ANALYSIS: Nacho Year vs Liquor Crickets

## Nacho Year Strengths
- **Starting Pitching Depth**: 7 SP with positive netValue (Luzardo, McLean, Pérez, Gausman, Cabrera, Lodolo, Fried)
- **Power Production**: Strong HR contributors (Kurtz 37.5, Marte 28.4, Rice 26.7, Langford 24.4)
- **Young SP Assets**: Multiple high-upside arms under 30 (McLean 25, Pérez 23, Cabrera 28, Lodolo 28)
- **Positional Balance**: Covered at premium positions (C, 1B, 2B, OF depth)
- **Strikeout Production**: Elite K numbers across SP staff (Luzardo 193, Gausman 174, Fried 170, Pérez 159)

## Nacho Year Weaknesses
- **Stolen Bases**: Critically weak - only Langford (22.8) and Crews (25.1) provide meaningful SB; total SB production ~64
- **Saves/Holds**: Zero closers with positive value; Vesia (2.1 SV, 21.9 HLD) is only reliever
- **Infield Defense**: Only 2B covered with value (Marte); no SS, no 3B
- **Age Risk**: Marte (33), Gausman (35), Fried (32) - aging core pieces
- **Protection Crunch**: 15 protectable players for 12 spots = forced to expose $20+ in value

## Their Trade Chips
- **SP Surplus**: Can trade from 7 deep SP rotation - Lodolo ($3.7), Cabrera ($6.7), Gausman ($6.9) most expendable
- **Max Fried**: High pDFL ($36) but only $3 netValue at $41 salary - negative contract leverage
- **Outfield Depth**: 3 OF with positive value (Langford, Abreu, Crews) - could move Abreu ($6.2) or Crews ($6.1)
- **Ben Rice**: Catcher with $13.6 netValue but C is shallow position - tradeable if getting premium return
- **Middle-Tier SPs**: McLean ($14.2) or Pérez ($9.9) could be shopped if getting elite hitter back

## What They're Looking For
- **STOLEN BASE HELP**: Desperately need SB - only meaningful production from 2 players
- **CONSOLIDATION TARGETS**: 2-for-1 or 3-for-2 upgrades to protect more value within 12-player limit
- **Middle Infield**: SS or 3B with positive value (currently have neither)
- **Relief Help**: Saves/Holds production - completely absent from roster
- **Elite Young Hitters**: Prefer youth to reduce age exposure (Marte 33, Gausman 35, Fried 32)

## Top 5 Targets for Crickets

1. **Nick Kurtz (1B, $35.8)** - Elite value at $4 salary; Nacho has Ben Rice at C who can cover 1B, making Kurtz potentially available; massive power (37.5 HR, 94.8 RBI); Crickets have Yandy Díaz at 1B but this is game-changing value

2. **Ketel Marte (2B, $17.4)** - Nacho might move him due to age (33) and needing younger assets; strong across all hitting categories; Crickets have Gleyber Torres at 2B ($14.3) so slight upgrade with more power/speed balance

3. **Jesús Luzardo (SP, $18.4)** - Nacho's SP surplus makes him available; elite K rate (193); Crickets could upgrade from Boyd/Ryan tier; age 29 fits competitive window better than Sale (37)

4. **Wyatt Langford (OF, $15.1)** - Nacho has 3 OF so could move one; 22.8 SB helps Crickets' speed; solid power/runs production; Crickets have OF depth but this is an upgrade over Ward

5. **Nolan McLean (SP, $14.2)** - Age 25, high K rate (149), dirt-cheap $4 salary; Nacho's SP depth makes him expendable; Crickets could trade Pivetta ($3.6) + prospect for this upgrade

## Top 5 Crickets Trade Chips

1. **Zach Neto (SS, $31.4)** - **PERFECT FIT**: Nacho has NO shortstop; elite SB (26.8) fills their biggest need; 5-category contributor; Crickets have Tovar as SS backup ($7.2); this is THE trade centerpiece

2. **Matt Chapman (3B, $14.0)** - **FILLS INFIELD GAP**: Nacho has NO third baseman; solid power (23.5 HR), runs, some speed (8.8 SB); Crickets have Royce Lewis at 3B ($4.5) as replacement

3. **Aroldis Chapman (CL, $12.0)** - **FILLS RELIEF NEED**: Nacho has ZERO closer value; 28.1 saves is exactly what they lack; Crickets have Estévez ($3.8) as SV backup

4. **Taylor Ward (OF, $20.0)** - Strong 5-category OF; Nacho needs speed (4.6 SB helps); Crickets have OF surplus (Rooker, Ward, Neto can play OF); power/runs/avg all solid

5. **Gleyber Torres (2B, $14.3)** - Slight downgrade from Marte but 7 years younger (30 vs 33); still good power/speed balance (5.5 SB); if Nacho wants to get younger while maintaining 2B production; frees up Marte for Crickets

## RECOMMENDED TRADE FRAMEWORK

**Crickets Send**: Zach Neto ($31.4) + Matt Chapman ($14.0) = $45.4 value  
**Crickets Receive**: Nick Kurtz ($35.8) + Jesús Luzardo ($18.4) = $54.2 value

**Why Nacho Does It**: Gets elite SS + 3B (filling two position gaps), adds 26.8 + 8.8 = 35.6 SB (solves speed crisis), consolidates from 15→13 protectable players  
**Why Crickets Does It**: Upgrades 1B massively, adds elite SP, receives $8.8 more value, still has Tovar/Lewis as IF depth
