## Plankton Weaknesses

- **Limited outfield depth**: Only 2 protectable OF (Schwarber, Greene) - need to start 3
- **First base weakness**: Only O'Hearn at 1B with modest production (14 HR, 60 RBI)
- **Aging core concern**: Schwarber (33), Marte (33), O'Hearn (33), Gallen (31), Castillo (34)
