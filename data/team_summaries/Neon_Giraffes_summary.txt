## Neon Giraffes Strengths
- **Starting Pitching Depth**: 4 quality SPs (Crochet, Cabrera, King, López) with strong strikeout numbers
- **Relief Pitching**: Strong closer/setup combo with Díaz, Hoffman, Romero, Morejon, Estrada
- **Power/Speed Balance**: Bo Bichette provides both categories, Torkelson adds power
- **Value Efficiency**: Exactly 12 protectable players with tight salary structure

## Neon Giraffes Weaknesses
- **Hitting Depth**: Only 3 hitters with positive value (Bichette, Torkelson, Laureano)
- **Positional Coverage**: Weak at C, 2B, 3B, and OF depth
- **Offensive Categories**: Limited RBI/R production beyond their top hitters
- **Age Concerns**: Díaz (31), Hoffman (32), Laureano (31) aging in key roles

## Their Trade Chips
- **Surplus Starting Pitching**: 4 SPs when most teams protect 2-3 - King or López expendable
- **Relief Depth**: 5 quality relievers - could move Hoffman, Romero, or Morejon
- **Aging Veterans**: Díaz and Hoffman have value but age concerns make them tradeable
- **Positional Flexibility**: Laureano (OF) could be moved if they get better hitting

## What They're Looking For
- **Quality Hitters**: Need 2-3 more protectable position players
- **Positional Filling**: C, 2B, 3B, additional OF help desperately needed  
- **Offensive Categories**: Players who contribute across HR/RBI/R/SB categories
- **Youth Upgrade**: Younger players to replace aging veterans while maintaining value
